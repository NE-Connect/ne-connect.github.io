1. Scope of Service

NE Connect ("Company", "We", "Us") specializes in building custom digital storefronts, zero-commission booking engines, and offline-ready management tools for eye clinics and hospitals in Assam. Our core service includes a custom-coded informational website with an integrated patient booking form that connects directly to the clinic's WhatsApp/backend.

Optional upgrades: Full offline Hospital Management Systems (HMS) and OPD workflow tools are available as separate, quoted projects and are not covered under the standard website booking agreement.

2. Ownership & Intellectual Property

  • Content Ownership: The clinic (Client) retains 100% ownership of all uploaded content, including doctor profiles, photographs, clinic logos, text descriptions, and all patient booking data collected via the website.
  • Backend & Code IP: NE Connect retains full intellectual property rights to the source code, booking API architecture, custom Flask backend, and the core technologies utilized to build and maintain the system.
  • Non-Compete: The code is not for resale or redistribution by the clinic. The license is exclusive to the specific clinic for the duration of the service.

3. Pricing & Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC)

Setup Fee (₹15,000): This covers the design, development, mobile-responsive optimization, integration of the zero-commission booking form, and deployment to the live web server.

Annual Maintenance Contract (₹10,000/year): This AMC covers the running costs and ongoing care of the digital platform. The ₹10,000/year covers:

  • Domain Renewal: Annual cost of the clinic's custom domain (e.g., clinicname.com).
  • SSL Security: Automatic renewal of SSL certificates ensuring the "padlock" icon remains active.
  • Cloud Hosting: Infrastructure fees (shared PythonAnywhere/cloud hosting) to ensure 24/7 uptime.
  • Minor Content Updates: Unlimited minor changes (e.g., changing a phone number, swapping a doctor's photo, updating clinic timings) throughout the year.
  • Priority Maintenance: Technical support for website bugs or security patches.
⚠️ Scope Creep Policy: Major structural changes (e.g., adding a completely new page, changing the booking form logic, integrating a full HMS, or entirely redesigning the layout) are NOT covered under the ₹10,000 AMC. These major feature requests will be quoted as separate projects at a discounted consulting rate for AMC clients.

4. Delivery & Revisions

Once we receive the clinic's required assets (name, address, logos, doctor photos, and contact info), we deliver a live preview link within 24-48 hours. The ₹15,000 setup fee includes 2 rounds of revisions (minor text and layout adjustments). Any revisions beyond 2 rounds are subject to a ₹2,000 per session consulting fee.

NE Connect utilizes GitHub Pages, Cloudflare CDN, and PythonAnywhere for infrastructure and routing. While we maintain strict uptime monitoring, we are not liable for outages or downtime caused by these third-party infrastructure providers. AMC includes troubleshooting and migrating the site to restore service in the event of a third-party outage.

1. Data We Collect (From the Clinic)

To build the custom website, we require the following public clinic information:

  • Clinic Name, Address, and Google Maps embed link.
  • Names, qualifications, and photographs of the treating doctors.
  • List of services offered (e.g., Cataract, Glaucoma, Retina).
  • Contact Phone Number and Email Address.

2. Patient Booking Data (The Zero-Commission Engine)

NE Connect does NOT store, sell, or share patient data.

  • When a patient submits a booking via the custom website, the data (Name, Phone, Date, and Reason) is transmitted directly to the clinic's WhatsApp or the NE Connect Admin Dashboard.
  • NE Connect acts as the data pipeline (API). We do not retain patient health records or sensitive medical details in our cloud infrastructure. The data belongs entirely to the clinic.

3. Data Retention

We retain the clinic's onboarding details and communication records for operational, tax, and compliance purposes. You may request to have your profile closed at any time; however, financial records and support chat logs may be retained as required by Indian law and for legitimate business record-keeping.

4. Public Nature

The informational pages of the custom website (doctor profiles, gallery, services, address) are designed to be publicly accessible on the internet to help patients find the clinic. Clinics should ensure they do not include private contact numbers or photos that they wish to keep off the public web.

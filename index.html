Questions Clinic Owners Ask
Why pay you when my pharma MR gives me a free site?
Pharma sites are static templates. You don't own the data, and if you switch brands, the site disappears. My system gives you complete digital ownership—no pharma banners, no call-centers taking your bookings.
How does the zero-commission booking work?
Patients fill a simple 5-field form on your custom site. The booking drops directly into your clinic's WhatsApp/backend. No third-party platform takes a cut. You keep 100% of your consultation fees.
Is the ₹15k setup + ₹10k AMC for one year?
Yes. The setup fee covers the custom coding, doctor profiles, gallery, and booking form. The AMC covers your domain, SSL security, hosting, and unlimited minor content updates (changing a phone number, swapping a doctor's photo, etc.).
